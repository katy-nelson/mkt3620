Hi, I'm Katy!

I am a college student who enjoys working with people, being creative, and finding new ways to grow both personally and professionally. I created this website to share more about my experiences, skills, interests, and future goals.

A Little About Me

I would describe myself as social, approachable, hardworking, and motivated. I enjoy environments where I can communicate with others, work as part of a team, and take on new challenges.

One thing that is important to me is building strong relationships with the people around me. Whether I am working on a group project, helping a customer, or working with coworkers, I try to be someone who is dependable, positive, and easy to work with.

I also enjoy having opportunities to be creative. I like marketing, social media, events, fashion, interior design, and projects where I can combine creativity with organization and communication.

Education

I am currently a college student continuing to build my education in areas that interest me both personally and professionally. Through my classes, I have had the opportunity to learn more about business, marketing, management, psychology, communication, and working with others.

I enjoy classes that allow me to connect what I am learning to real-world situations. I especially enjoy projects where I can use communication, creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

My Strengths

Some of my biggest strengths are communication, teamwork, creativity, adaptability, and relationship building. I enjoy talking with people and making others feel comfortable, which has helped me in customer service, team projects, leadership roles, and professional environments.

Communication

I enjoy communicating with others and making connections. I have developed these skills through customer service, teamwork, presentations, internships, and everyday experiences.

Teamwork

I like being part of a team and contributing wherever I am needed. I try to listen to other people's ideas, stay flexible, and help the group reach its goal.

Creativity

I enjoy projects that give me the opportunity to come up with ideas, create content, design materials, or think about how something can be presented in a more interesting way.

My Interests

Outside of school and work, I enjoy staying involved and spending time with other people. I like activities that keep me social, creative, and active.

Some of my interests include fashion, social media, marketing, interior design, events, sports, spending time with friends and family, and finding new creative projects to work on.

My Goals

One of my biggest goals is to continue gaining experience that helps me better understand what I want to do professionally. I want to keep building my communication, leadership, sales, marketing, and problem-solving skills.

I hope to have a career that allows me to work closely with people, build relationships, stay creative, and continue learning. I am excited to keep gaining new experiences and see where those opportunities take me.
