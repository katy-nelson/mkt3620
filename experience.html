My Experience

My past jobs and internships have given me experience in marketing, customer service, research, leadership, social media, events, and working closely with people. Each position has helped me build skills that I can carry into future opportunities.

Marketing & Events

Marketing and Events Coordinator Intern

One of my most valuable experiences has been working as a Marketing and Events Coordinator Intern for a Chamber of Commerce. This role gave me hands-on experience with marketing, event planning, social media, and community outreach.

I helped create and manage social media content to promote local businesses, community events, and Chamber initiatives. I also assisted with planning, coordinating, and promoting events such as Sweet Corn Days, Downtown Market, and Lead the Ville.

Through this internship, I created promotional materials, helped with advertisements, worked with local businesses, and supported event logistics. It showed me how much planning and communication goes into successfully promoting an event or organization.

Interior Design & Social Media

Interior Design Company Internship

I have also gained experience working with an interior design company, where I have been able to combine creativity with marketing and social media.

I have helped create social media content featuring design projects, job site visits, mood boards, before-and-after transformations, staging, remodeling projects, and behind-the-scenes moments.

This experience has helped me better understand brand voice, visual marketing, storytelling, and how social media can be used to showcase a business in a way that feels authentic and engaging.

Research Experience

Research Assistant

As a research assistant, I gained experience working with participant recruitment, data organization, troubleshooting, and Excel.

I helped organize research data, complete coding tasks, communicate with participants, and assist with different parts of the research process. This experience strengthened my attention to detail and showed me the importance of accuracy, organization, and professionalism.

Customer Service

Coffee Shop Team Lead

Working in a coffee shop gave me strong experience in customer service, teamwork, communication, and working efficiently in a fast-paced environment.

I prepared coffee, espresso, and specialty drinks while making sure customers received friendly and personalized service. As a team lead, I also helped support coworkers, answer questions, solve problems, and keep the team organized during busy shifts.

Server

My serving experience taught me how to stay organized while managing multiple responsibilities at once. I focused on creating positive and memorable experiences for guests while maintaining accuracy and professionalism.

I also trained new servers on menu knowledge, service procedures, and customer service expectations. Training others helped me strengthen my communication and leadership skills.

Childcare Experience

Working in childcare helped me learn patience, responsibility, and communication. I built positive relationships with children and families and helped maintain a safe, clean, and organized environment.

Leadership & Teamwork

Many of my experiences have involved working closely with other people. I enjoy being part of a team and contributing wherever I can.

Through roles such as team lead, server trainer, intern, research assistant, and student organization member, I have learned how important communication, flexibility, and reliability are when working toward a shared goal.

I try to be someone who is approachable and dependable while also being willing to take initiative when something needs to get done.
